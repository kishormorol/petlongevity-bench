Main contribution

Evaluate scope-preserving scientific synthesis.

The benchmark does not reward a polished answer merely because it sounds plausible. It requires a model to ground a claim in exact evidence, preserve its scientific scope, and state uncertainty when the paper cannot support the requested conclusion.

Benchmark design

Four ways a research system can fail—or earn trust.

Task-level reporting makes failures inspectable. The benchmark does not collapse incompatible errors into one headline score.

M1 · EVIDENCE

Extract

Recover structured study evidence with an exact source span and penalize unsupported fields.

citation & species errors
M2 · JUDGMENT

Verify

Distinguish supported, contradicted, and insufficient claims while preserving study scope.

causal & transfer overclaim
M3 · TIME

Prioritize

Rank hypotheses using a frozen historical record rather than future knowledge.

post-cutoff leakage
M4 · FORECAST

Forecast

Evaluate biomarker predictions with direction and cross-species concordance.

correlation as causation

Results registry

Verified, reproducible system runs.

An entry is eligible for rank only when its traceable run manifest and required checks are complete.

Interpretation boundary. Benchmark performance does not establish veterinary efficacy, safety, or clinical utility. M3 results require a frozen pre-cutoff source snapshot; cross-species evidence is not direct evidence.

Contribute

Submit a system run to the registry.

Add a complete machine-readable record to leaderboard/submissions.jsonl, rebuild the portal, then request verification. Self-reported results remain pending and unranked.

Required: stable system/version identity, B0–B3 baseline, evaluated modules, primary metric and score, dataset version, timestamp, and run-manifest SHA-256.

PetLongevity-Bench evaluates research systems. It does not provide veterinary prescribing, dosing, or unsupervised study guidance.
